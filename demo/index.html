TurboPush demo.

Pick some files and watch them go up in 1 MB chunks, four at a time, with retries, pause, resume and cancel.

Running on your machine

Uploads go to upload.php in this folder, which stores them under your system temp directory, never in the project.

  • Chunks of 1 MB, within PHP's default upload limit
  • Four chunk requests at a time
  • Three retries per chunk, after 1, 2 and 4 seconds
